## Design Context

### Users

Tammy serves Australian small-business owners and bookkeepers who need to set up and maintain accurate local accounts without a cloud account. Their core journey is create or reopen an encrypted workspace, configure the business, review source records, post and reconcile transactions, prepare GST/BAS drafts, and understand what needs attention.

### Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and precise. The product should feel like a capable Australian accounting desk: friendly enough for an owner-operator, rigorous enough for a bookkeeper, and explicit whenever a workflow is draft, local-only, or requires human approval.

### Aesthetic Direction

A refined light desktop interface with warm off-white surfaces, deep forest green identity and actions, restrained borders, compact data tables, and clear serif accents for the Tammy wordmark. Preserve the existing application mock-up's quiet density and avoid generic SaaS gradients, glass effects, oversized metrics, excessive cards, dark-mode theatre, or decorative motion.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next accounting action obvious without hiding provenance or status.
2. Prefer compact, well-labelled desktop layouts and semantic tables over decorative dashboards.
3. Use calm hierarchy and precise language; never imply BAS lodgement, automation, or external assurance that does not exist.
4. Keep setup, privacy, errors, empty states, and recovery understandable without specialist accounting knowledge.
5. Meet WCAG AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, form labels, status announcements, and reduced-motion preferences.
