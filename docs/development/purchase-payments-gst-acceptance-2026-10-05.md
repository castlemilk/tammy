## Implemented behavior covered by the acceptance cases

Phase two adds a human-confirmed purchase accounting path to Documents and purchase source review to GST & BAS. A reviewed local document is still only a candidate: the operator confirms ordered account/use/tax/cash-flow allocations before posting. Posting recognizes the expense and payable once. Later payments settle the payable and retain their actual entered dates; they do not recognize the expense a second time.

For cash GST, only paid eligible portions enter the selected claim period; a $110 gross/$10 GST source paid in two $55 instalments can produce the respective $5 credits in the two quarters. Non-cash GST is attributable once on the supported invoice-date basis, independent of later settlement. Private, non-creditable, capital and mixed-use allocations remain distinct in the retained sources and cash-flow facts. Suspense, unsupported evidence, explicit future deferral, attribution, duplicate/prior-accounting outcomes, and legacy review remain visible as typed decisions rather than silently becoming a credit.

Saved BAS workpapers retain their source and review rows. Later payments, evidence changes, attributions, deferrals, and new review outcomes can stale a draft even where its eligible total is unchanged. Export actions serialize the displayed retained Core workpaper; a stale export does not silently regenerate it. The purchase journeys save actual CSV/PDF outputs, compare them independently to the exact Core response sidecars, and exercise regeneration and reopen after restart. The independent CSV/PDF checks include source and review identities, hashes, exact cents, invoice/payment/claim dates, classifications, and all bounded rows. The .core.pb files beside the CSV evidence are serialized from the exact BasWorkpaperSchema response used by that export. Named Core sidecars accompany opening-reviewed, future-claim, claimed-and-reviewed, reviewed-through-today, complete, overflow-retained, first-quarter, second-quarter, retained-stale and restarted CSV/PDF pairs.

The UI provides opening-payable source review without inventing a document identity. The packaged opening-without-document journey uses a test-only, encrypted-workspace fixture to create that historical source. A separate earlier-period manual cash journal is created through production Core commands. This validates source discovery and review; it does not add an opening-settlement or credit workflow.

The package covers cash, non-cash, and mixed/private/capital purchase paths, retained cash-flow facts, evidence/suspense/deferral review, source and payment pagination, restart, keyboard-retained details, and saved exports. The six purchase journeys are included in the existing package suite. Authentication, forbidden roles, closed/advance dates, stale versions, exact replay, changed-key replay, duplicate prevention, and rollback remain covered by real encrypted Core/TLS integration tests; the UI package cases do not replay every negative Core case through a screen.

## Scope limits

It does not provide a supplier register, broad bill approval, supplier credits, opening-payable settlement, bank matching or transfer, aged payables, refunds, reversals/corrections, BAS preparation/freeze, PAYG coverage, declaration, ATO/SBR lodgement, or production transport. The opening-source test fixture is not an operator workflow. Phase three must add completeness coverage and lifecycle controls without rediscovering purchase credits from document pages.
