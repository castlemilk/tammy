# Local BAS preparation, review, and manual handoff

Status: implementation authorised by the user's 2026-10-05 programme continuation instruction; see [execution plan](../plans/2026-10-05-bas-preparation-lifecycle.md). ATO submission remains outside scope.

The requested work has three connected parts: local income and BAS PDF/CSV exports, purchase payments with cash-basis GST credits, and a complete local BAS preparation workflow. This specification owns the third part and describes its dependencies on the first two. The proposed first release covers Simpler GST plus supported PAYG withholding and PAYG instalments, subject to the user's written design approval.

## 1. Outcome and supported profile

A user can select the exact reporting period, resolve missing source evidence, review the applicable GST and PAYG labels, freeze the reviewed figures for manual external handling, export that exact version, record what they did outside Tammy, and prepare a linked revision without altering the original. Restart and encrypted backup/restore preserve the complete chain.

Tammy prepares local reports. It does not connect to the ATO, obtain ATO prefill, lodge a BAS, authenticate an official receipt, or control an ATO account. Installing an SBR credential never changes this boundary. The screen and exports distinguish a draft, a frozen manual handoff, and a user-recorded external confirmation.

The initial supported profile is:

- A user-confirmed, effective-dated GST registration and Simpler BAS reporting method, with supported cash or non-cash attribution.
- Ordinary monthly or quarterly reporting periods, confirmed against the business's actual activity statement. The initial complete-statement path requires the supported obligations to share the statement's reporting window. Mixed obligation cadences require separate authoritative period handling and remain blocked until implemented.
- GST labels G1, 1A and 1B from supported, fully reconciled sources.
- Small or medium PAYG withholding, with reviewed payroll/withholding evidence for W1, W2, W3 and W4, and the resulting W5/4. Tammy does not calculate withholding from salary totals. Statement-specific reporting requirements, including any official prefill, must be explicitly confirmed rather than inferred.
- PAYG instalments using the ATO-notified amount or notified rate without a variation. Inputs come from retained ATO-statement evidence and supported period income facts; the root business-income figure is comparison information, never an automatic T1 value.

An active unsupported obligation, an unknown applicability answer, or an unsupported GST transaction prevents freezing a complete statement. Draft GST workpapers and a clearly incomplete reviewer export remain available. The unsupported matrix includes large PAYG withholders, PAYG variations/credits, GST full reporting, GST instalments, annual GST returns, unsupported mixed cadences, FBT instalments, WET, LCT, fuel tax credits, deferred company instalments, deferred/import GST and other special GST schemes or adjustments without implemented attribution rules. These cases show their specific missing support rather than being converted into zeros.

This is a complete workflow for the supported profile, not a claim that every Australian BAS or tax obligation is supported.

## 2. Existing foundation and boundaries

The current `TaxService` in `proto/tammy/v1/tax.proto` exposes only `CreateBasDraft` and `GetCurrentBasDraft`. `services/core/internal/reporting/bas_repository.go` saves immutable workpaper rows and source rows; migration `0006_banking_reporting.sql` prohibits updating or deleting them. Regeneration creates another ID. Migration `0011_bas_save_order.sql` determines the latest committed snapshot independently of clocks. These guarantees remain intact.

`services/core/internal/reporting/bas_sources.go` consumes invoice postings/receipts and the complete phase-two purchase-owned ReportingPort snapshot: reconciled cash payments/non-cash claims, source evidence, explicit attribution/deferral, unresolved/excluded/unsupported/legacy dispositions, and document/opening-payable provenance. Cash credits now follow eligible paid portions; non-cash claims remain one-time. See [phase-two purchase acceptance](../../development/purchase-payments-gst-acceptance-2026-10-05.md) and [purchase source handoff](../../development/purchase-reporting-sources.md). The phase-three complete coverage manifest is still required: the current port does not consume manual TaxFacts and does not prove that every cross-domain source domain and obligation is classified. Those facts cannot silently disappear from a complete BAS.

`proto/tammy/v1/organisation.proto` supplies current GST basis, frequency, financial-year end and a rule reference, but no obligation or registration history. GST-setting updates require a future date, yet the current profile is updated immediately and the invoice attribution boundary rejects unsupported historical basis changes. This release preserves that rejection unless a separate historical attribution design is implemented.

The company-return contracts and `services/core/internal/annualreporting/transitions.go` provide useful lifecycle, version, warning, declaration and amendment patterns. They are not a working report persistence or manual handoff implementation. No local confirmation will reuse `CompanyReturnSubmissionReceipt` or its `DELIVERED` authority, which is reserved for official accepted submission evidence.

## 3. Effective-dated obligations and reporting calendar

Add a BAS-owned obligations profile linked to the organisation, with immutable revisions and an effective start/end interval. It records registration status and dates, user-confirmed Simpler/full/instalment reporting method, supported turnover category, GST basis and cadence, PAYGW category/cadence, PAYGI method/cadence, and explicit applicability for each other activity-statement obligation. Retain the actor, recorded time, reason, evidence references and profile hash. A missing answer is `UNKNOWN`; `NOT_APPLICABLE` is an explicit reviewed assertion. An active obligation with a genuine zero amount is not `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Generate candidate monthly and Australian July–September, October–December, January–March and April–June periods in the workspace civil-date timezone. Financial-year end does not move GST quarters. Historical snapshots pin the profile revision effective for their period. A new profile revision affects its effective interval and future drafts; it never rewrites a frozen statement or retroactively supplies missing history.

The user confirms the actual statement's period, applicable sections and displayed due date. Store the due date separately from the tax period, together with whether it is the normal suggested date or a user-recorded external date/deferral. Suggested dates are build/rule-versioned and labelled as guidance. Do not automatically grant online, agent, disaster or other extensions. Registration start/end part-periods, cadence changes, annual methods and mixed obligation windows need implemented rules or an explicit unsupported result. Retain the PAYGI method choice and the confirmed income-year identity; the first activity statement's selected method applies for the rest of that income year. Do not silently change it because another method appears easier in a later period.

Retain one statement family per organisation and confirmed actual statement period/obligation-window scope, with an original and linked replacements/revisions. This is a stable statement identity: profile revision, profile hash, calculation revision and rule version are not family-key fields. A corrected obligations profile for an existing statement belongs to a replacement/revision within the same family. One atomic family-head/version and successor constraint governs original confirmations, replacements and revisions alike. The calendar shows every retained period and its local state; the organisation-wide latest-workpaper pointer is a convenience, not report identity.

## 4. Sources, calculation and completeness

Build a BAS source snapshot in one SQLCipher read transaction through module-owned ports for invoice sales, purchase settlements/credits, supported journal tax facts, reviewed PAYG facts, profile and rule metadata. Capture exact IDs, versions, content hashes and tax dates. Retain the revision vector as provenance. Staleness fingerprints a coverage manifest containing every relevant candidate and its classification, eligibility, exclusion reason, evidence, unresolved/unsupported status and reviewed disposition, alongside eligible amounts, profile and rule metadata. Include purchase deferrals, legacy review/payment-history states and source links. A newly excluded, unsupported or unresolved fact must invalidate review even if eligible totals are unchanged. Report-only persistence and export events do not enter that manifest, so saving or exporting a report cannot make itself stale.

An issued invoice whose supported tax date is attributable to this statement but which lacks its required accounting posting is a completeness blocker, escalating the existing GST-workpaper warning. It cannot be silently excluded to freeze a complete statement. Draft invoices remain outside posted income and are explained separately. Tests introduce unposted and newly unresolved sources after review, including unchanged-total cases, and prove that freeze fails.

The purchase-payment feature must supply immutable document/payment allocations and creditable GST, including business-use/eligibility evidence. Cash credits follow qualifying payment dates, including payments for documents from earlier periods. Non-cash credits follow the implemented attribution rule. Never substitute a payment amount for a tax invoice or credit entitlement. The purchase source contract must include document version/hash, payment ID/hash when applicable, allocation and tax date; the current document-only `BasSourceLine` is insufficient for that provenance.

Manual GST journals either enter through a typed tax-fact projection with explicit sales/purchase/adjustment classification and supported timing, or create a blocker. Use the source identity/lineage to prevent counting an invoice posting, document, settlement and journal representation of the same event more than once. A manually entered aggregate cannot override calculated G1/1A/1B or conceal an unreconciled tax fact. Unsupported reversals, refunds, credit notes, prior-period adjustments or schemes remain visible blockers until their module owns a supported correction fact.

PAYG inputs are typed, retained evidence facts. Each applicable label has a known amount/value or a reviewed zero; inactive sections have explicit not-applicable answers. Required evidence can remain explicitly `UNKNOWN` while preparing, but blocks completion. PAYGW facts reference the relevant payroll/withholding summary and reconcile W5 to W2 + W3 + W4, then label 4 to W5, subject to the confirmed statement requirements. PAYGI amount-method evidence pins the notified T7 amount, copied to 5A. Rate-method evidence pins notified T2 and user-reviewed T1 instalment income; T11 is calculated as T1 × T2 and copied to 5A. Represent the rate as an exact bounded decimal/rational, never a floating-point estimate, and retain an inspectable calculation trace. The reviewed reportable T1, exact rate and resulting whole-dollar T11 must follow the pinned official instructions. A previously varied value reflected in a current ATO notice is retained as historical provenance, not described as a newly initiated variation. Only a confirmed currently operative value within the supported non-variation method is accepted; a remaining need to complete variation fields, claim 5B or interpret an uncertain prior variation blocks completion. Tammy does not initiate variations in this release.

Keep all workpaper amounts in exact integer AUD cents. Convert atomic activity-statement labels to whole dollars by omitting cents without rounding up, at the label boundary rather than per transaction. Then derive form aggregates from the reported whole-dollar labels: W5 = reported W2 + W3 + W4, label 4 = reported W5, 8A = reported 1A + 4 + 5A, and 8B = reported 1B for this supported profile. Do not separately truncate an exact-cent W5 or summary total, because that can disagree with the form's component equations. Label 9 is the nonnegative magnitude of reported 8A minus reported 8B, with a separate `PAYABLE`, `REFUNDABLE` or `NIL` direction; never export a negative label 9. Retain and show the independent exact-cent workpaper/calculation trace, reportable values and differences. An inactive section contributes only through an explicit applicability decision. Other credit/debit labels cannot be ignored to make this formula appear complete.

Validation produces stable typed `BLOCKER`, `WARNING`, `INFORMATION` and `UNSUPPORTED` outcomes with source links. Blockers include unsupported obligations/methods, unknown profile or PAYG answers, invalid period, source-bound overflow, missing historical attribution/evidence, duplicate facts, incomplete tax-fact coverage, unmatched accounting/GST totals, unreconciled source changes and arithmetic limits. A source-volume limit cannot be worked around by shortening the actual activity-statement period; use complete bounded/paginated capture or report an unsupported volume. Warning acknowledgements bind to the exact validation revision and source snapshot; they do not waive blockers. Unpaid cash purchases with no qualifying settlement are explained exclusions, while a qualifying payment whose credit entitlement cannot be established requires review. A nil statement is explicit and passes the same completeness checks.

## 5. Persisted local lifecycle

Use a new mutable BAS preparation aggregate that references immutable workpaper/calculation versions. Keep existing workpaper rows and their `DRAFT_NOT_LODGED` status unchanged; they describe captured source figures, not the new workflow authority.

| From | Allowed result | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `COLLECTING` | `BLOCKED`, `REVIEW_READY` | Deterministic validation of the current revision. |
| `BLOCKED` | `COLLECTING`, `REVIEW_READY` | Inputs are resolved and the revision is validated again. |
| `REVIEW_READY` | `COLLECTING`, `BLOCKED`, `FROZEN_FOR_MANUAL_HANDOFF` | An edit/source change invalidates review; freezing requires current successful validation and acknowledgements. |
| `FROZEN_FOR_MANUAL_HANDOFF` | `EXTERNAL_CONFIRMATION_RECORDED`, `SUPERSEDED_BEFORE_CONFIRMATION`, `SUPERSEDED_AFTER_RECORDED_REJECTION` | A user records completed external lodgement/acceptance for this exact snapshot, explicitly states it is unused and creates a replacement, or authorises a new attempt after a resolved user-recorded rejection. |
| `EXTERNAL_CONFIRMATION_RECORDED` | `SUPERSEDED_BY_CONFIRMED_REVISION` | A linked revision obtains its own user-recorded completed external lodgement/acceptance for its exact snapshot. |
| Any superseded state | none | Retained terminal predecessor/attempt. |

Export events are independent history: exporting does not confirm submission, acceptance, payment or a refund. `FROZEN_FOR_MANUAL_HANDOFF` is already immutable before an export succeeds. A cancelled or failed export leaves the same frozen report available for retry.

Freeze pins the organisation/profile, exact source/calculation hashes, cent workpaper, whole-dollar labels, validation revision/acknowledgements, form/rule/calculator/schema/descriptor versions and a review approval record. Perform expected-version, source-currentness and role/fresh-factor checks in the commit transaction. The renderer cannot grant readiness or submit a fabricated approval marker.

While collecting, regeneration creates a new immutable calculation/workpaper ID under the same family and clears prior validation acknowledgements. Frozen reports remain retrievable by ID even when sources change. Show the changed-source warning without recalculating their saved labels. A new source fact, organisation/profile change or rule change cannot overwrite a frozen version. Use a linked replacement before external use or a linked revision after recorded external confirmation.

If the user reports that external handling is pending or its outcome is unknown, retain that append-only observation and block a claim that the predecessor was unused. The user resolves it through an explicit subsequent external observation; Tammy does not poll the ATO or guess success. Creating a revision does not itself supersede the confirmed predecessor.

## 6. Manual confirmation and linked revisions

`ExternalBASConfirmation` is a separate user-asserted record: frozen BAS ID/hash, event kind, external channel, claimed event date/time, optional external reference, bounded evidence object/hash, actor and core-recorded time. Its visible trust classification is always `USER_RECORDED_UNVERIFIED`. Evidence such as an uploaded receipt does not change that classification. A payment receipt is not lodging/acceptance evidence, and a reviewer handoff is not external lodgement.

The action says **Record external confirmation** and the status says **External confirmation recorded by [user]**. It never says **Lodged by Tammy**, **ATO accepted**, **Official receipt verified** or uses an SBR delivered state. Only an explicit user-asserted completed external lodgement or acceptance event, bound to this exact frozen snapshot, can advance `EXTERNAL_CONFIRMATION_RECORDED` and the local effective chain. Pending, unknown, rejection, reviewer handoff and payment/refund events remain observations; they cannot advance that state or supersede a predecessor. Rejection leaves the frozen snapshot intact with a review-needed observation.

Correct an erroneous confirmation through a reasoned append-only correction that retains the original and its evidence. A correction never silently changes the predecessor/successor chain. If it disputes a qualifying confirmation, visibly flag that confirmation and its dependent chain as disputed and block further effective-chain advancement. A later rejection contradicting an already qualifying confirmation opens the same dispute; rejection before any qualifying confirmation remains an observation. Preserve the chain and every observation as history.

`ResolveExternalBASDispute` requires the BAS approver action, its own fresh TOTP purpose `bas_resolve_external_dispute`, expected family version/head ID/hash, the exact disputed observations and every affected dependent confirmed revision, bounded evidence references and reason. It supports two deterministic outcomes. `UPHOLD_RECORDED_CHAIN` appends a reviewed resolution and clears the dispute overlay without changing the retained head or rewriting any confirmation. `REQUIRE_CORRECTIVE_REVISION` retains the existing head and disputed history, records `CORRECTION_REQUIRED`, and reserves one active corrective branch rooted at that head. Unrelated confirmations/replacements remain blocked until that branch obtains its own qualifying user-recorded external confirmation. That command advances the head atomically and links the resolution to the retained affected chain. Changed head/dependencies or unresolved conflicting observations reject the resolution. This first release does not rewind or delete recorded chains; a requested withdrawal requiring such a rewind remains explicitly unsupported. Preparation states and dispute overlays are distinct, so resolving a dispute never edits frozen labels or reopens a superseded predecessor.

Upheld resolutions preserve an existing branch's review and frozen workflow; adopting a branch or adding a frozen barrier belongs only to a corrective resolution. Conflicting assertions can also arise before the first confirmed head, including late reported use of an unused/rejected predecessor or competing rejection/confirmation assertions on the active frozen attempt. An upheld resolution then pins explicit head absence and an empty confirmed dependency manifest, retains every disputed assertion, and preserves the active attempt. It never creates a confirmation or revives a predecessor; the active attempt requires its own fresh qualifying observation to create the first head. A corrective revision requires an existing confirmed head. Historical resolutions continue to validate against the confirmed dependency prefix at their Core append ordinal after later confirmations.

One active corrective branch may contain multiple immutable attempts. A frozen attempt confirmed unused can be replaced through the existing purpose-bound unused assertion; it becomes `SUPERSEDED_BEFORE_CONFIRMATION` and a new collecting attempt inherits the same branch reservation and root head. After user-recorded rejection, an explicit `ReplaceRejectedBASAttempt` command requires the BAS approver, fresh purpose `bas_replace_rejected_attempt`, expected family/branch/attempt versions, the resolved rejection observation and reason. It retains the rejected attempt as `SUPERSEDED_AFTER_RECORDED_REJECTION` and creates a collecting successor in the same branch. Pending, unknown, contradictory or disputed external outcomes cannot use this recovery path. Every replacement advances the branch version atomically; only the current active frozen attempt can obtain a qualifying confirmation, and only one effective successor can advance the family head. The same retained-attempt recovery applies to ordinary replacement/revision branches. No attempt overwrites another's labels or evidence.

A linked revision captures its root statement ID, latest confirmed predecessor ID/hash/version, the same period, reason and the newly captured source/profile/rule revision. It displays original/revised label amounts and deltas. It follows the same collect, validate, review, freeze, export and confirmation workflow. Only its own qualifying completed external lodgement/acceptance confirmation advances the local effective chain; a rejected or pending revision never supersedes the predecessor. Expected latest-predecessor version and a single effective-successor constraint prevent two concurrent revisions from independently becoming current.

A change to a transaction that was correct when originally reported may require a later-period adjustment; it is not automatically a revision of the old statement. Tammy does not automatically carry historical differences into the next period or correct the same fact twice. This first release prepares a linked same-period revision where supported; adjustment/correction treatment outside that model is flagged for external review.

## 7. Contracts, storage and security

Add generated, organisation-scoped messages/RPCs for obligations profile Get/Set/history, reporting-period listing/confirmation, BAS preparation Get/List by ID, typed PAYG input/evidence updates, source/calculation regeneration, validation/warning acknowledgement, freeze, linked replacement/revision, external observation/confirmation/correction, `ResolveExternalBASDispute` and `ReplaceRejectedBASAttempt`. Mutations carry `CommandContext`, canonical UUID idempotency key, expected aggregate version and exact relevant snapshot/validation hash. Family-changing commands additionally carry the expected family version/head identity; attempt changes carry the expected branch/active-attempt versions. Queries use `AuthenticationContext` and bounded pagination. Current-workpaper lookup remains backward compatible; exports and deep links always use an exact persisted ID.

Add encrypted tables for immutable obligation revisions, statement families/preparation aggregates, calculation revisions, typed facts and evidence links, validation revisions/acknowledgements, frozen approvals/snapshot hashes, external observations and export metadata. Enforce unique identity/lineage and immutable history constraints in storage. Export file bytes are generated from captured data and metadata, not current source queries or renderer-supplied labels. Report artifacts and evidence survive existing-workspace migration and backup/restore; restored sessions/factors remain governed by current identity policy.

Reuse the ordinary command coordinator in `services/core/internal/app/command.go` for transactional authorisation, canonical replay/conflict, results, source changes and audit. The current BAS handler in `services/core/internal/localproduct/ledger_sqlcipher.go` is not the lifecycle authority and should delegate to a focused BAS service rather than accumulating state logic. Bind an actual durable audit appender: the existing `localCommandAuditor` is a no-op, so coordinator use alone does not establish retained audit. Append canonical events for profile decisions, source capture, inputs, validation, approval, replacement/revision, confirmation/correction and completed exports. Audit payloads retain identifiers, hashes and bounded reason/status metadata, not evidence bytes, payroll contents, secrets or destination paths.

Read access follows `read_financial`; preparation/inputs/validation follow `prepare_tax`. Freeze requires an explicit BAS approval action granted to `business_lodger`, with purpose-bound fresh TOTP assertion `bas_freeze_manual_handoff`; workspace administration alone does not grant approval authority. A sole operator can hold both roles. External confirmation and predecessor-not-used assertions require the relevant BAS approver action and their own purpose-bound fresh assertions. The new purpose is issued through existing `AssertTOTP`, revalidated against the actual actor/session and atomically consumed inside the BAS command. The five-minute limit, replay rejection and rollback behavior remain those of `services/core/internal/identity/service.go` and `authorisation.ValidateFreshFactor`; do not build a report-specific fake challenge or substitute a workspace passphrase. This local approval records reviewed handoff, not an ATO declaration or authority to lodge directly.

Use the trusted-main export boundary demonstrated by `apps/desktop/src/main/invoice-export.ts`: persisted ID input, authenticated core snapshot, session/workspace guard, native save dialog, bounded local rendering, private temporary file, sync and atomic activation, cancellation cleanup, and no path returned to React. The export dependency owns reusable PDF/CSV mechanics, sanitisation and export completion auditing. The BAS lifecycle supplies exact frozen snapshot/manifest data and draft/incomplete watermarks.

## 8. Desktop workflow

Extend `apps/desktop/src/app/features/bas/bas-screen.tsx` into a restrained table-led workbench with a period/history selector and five visible stages: **Prepare**, **Review**, **Freeze**, **Export**, **External record**. The preparation summary identifies confirmed profile, applicable obligations, basis, period and due-date source.

GST tables retain source drill-down and exact cents. PAYGW/PAYGI tables show each required input, provenance, known zero/not-applicable decision, and unresolved review. The review table pairs each reportable label with its exact workpaper amount, whole-dollar value, evidence and any rounding difference. Summary distinguishes net GST from the complete supported-statement payable/refundable amount, so PAYG is not hidden inside a GST figure.

Blockers provide a direct route to the missing input or unsupported case. Freeze opens the exact review summary and the existing purpose-bound authentication flow. Frozen views show their ID, hash/version and source-change indication, with exports for that exact version. History keeps cancelled exports, predecessor links and every external observation. A linked revision view shows original/revised/difference columns. Overview points to exact BAS IDs and distinguishes action needed, ready for review, frozen for external handling and user-recorded external confirmation. No button says **Submit to ATO**.

## 10. Official references and open approval decisions

ATO Simpler BAS reduces the GST information to G1, 1A and 1B; it does not remove other applicable activity-statement obligations. Sources: [ATO software developer Simpler BAS guidance](https://softwaredevelopers.ato.gov.au/SimplerBAS), [ATO activity-statement obligation sections](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/business-activity-statements-bas), and [ATO PAYG withholding labels](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/preparing-lodging-and-paying/business-activity-statements-bas/pay-as-you-go-payg-withholding).

The ATO directs users to enter whole dollars without rounding cents up: [ATO BAS preparation tips](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/0-30bd4b2d-40c7-42bb-984a-adf4ee2fa327). The current amount/rate instructions and statement show T7 → 5A or T1 × T2 → T11 → 5A, and separate payment/refund direction: [ATO PAYGI instructions](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/0-c9dc388e-c500-43a2-863b-bbdc01b6ff3a), [ATO sample activity statement](https://www.ato.gov.au/api/public/content/533b1e6c-2d50-49ef-a7cf-b2b6e28acbb4_CAS27842BASU_pdf). A GST error, later credit claim and adjustment are distinct and cannot be corrected twice: [ATO definition of GST errors](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/in-detail/managing-gst-in-your-business/reporting-paying-and-activity-statements/correcting-gst-errors/what-is-a-gst-error).
